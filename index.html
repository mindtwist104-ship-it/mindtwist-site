MindTwist Studio Manager

A private publishing tool for the MindTwist YouTube channel · Uses YouTube API Services · Privacy Policy

What it is

MindTwist Studio Manager is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the MindTwist YouTube channel to publish and manage the channel's own original videos. It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else, and it has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's own finished long-form videos and Shorts, with a scheduled publish time
  • Sets each video's thumbnail
  • Uploads accurate captions generated from the channel's own scripts
  • Adds videos to the channel's own playlists
  • Posts one creator comment on the channel's own videos
  • Reads the channel's own analytics (views, retention, click-through rate) to improve future videos

What it does not do

  • It does not access any other channel or any other user's data
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties
  • It does not download or reuse other creators' content

How access works

The channel owner signs in once with Google (OAuth 2.0) and grants the tool access to their own channel. The resulting access credential is stored encrypted and is used only to perform the actions listed above. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
